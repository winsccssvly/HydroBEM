# Error map of the finite-depth Green function tables

Generated by `scripts/gen_green_finite_tables.py` (do not edit).

The tables give the coefficients $Q_m(w, H)$ of the decomposition of the finite-depth Green
function for $R/h < 1/2$, $Q = \sum_{m=0}^{9} s^m Q_m(w, H)$ (theory §8.2), with
$s = (R/h)^2$, $w = (v/h)^2 \in [0, 4]$ and $H = Kh$. Each $Q_m$ is a Chebyshev interpolant
of degree 12 in $w$ and, per region, of degree 14 in $\tau = \log H$ for
$10^{-12} \le H \le 50$ and of degree 8 in $t = 1/H$ for $H \ge 50$.

The error of a fit is the largest over the test points of $\sum_m 4^{-m} \lvert \Delta Q_m \rvert$,
for the values and for the derivatives $\partial Q_m / \partial \sqrt{w}$, with the tolerances
1e-09 and 1e-08. The reference is a verified double-precision contour quadrature
(20 against 30 Gauss nodes per panel, cross-checked against mpmath); its own error estimate is
the last column of Table 1.

## Frequency limits

Below $H = 10^{-12}$ and at $\omega = 0$ the tables use the limit
$\ell_m(w) = \lim_{H \to 0} (Q_m + \delta_{m0} \log(H)/2)$, interpolated in $w$ of degree
12: its error is 4.5e-14 (values) and 2.5e-12 (derivatives). The remainder of the
limit, which is $O(H \log H)$, is 7.3e-07 at $H = 10^{-8}$, 9.2e-09 at $H = 10^{-10}$, 1.1e-10 at $H = 10^{-12}$.

At $\omega = \infty$ the region in $t$ at $t = 0$ is the limit $H \to \infty$ of $Q_m$: it
differs from the directly computed limit by 4.5e-14.

## Modified Bessel functions

$[1/2, 48)$. The degree is raised until the largest relative error is below 1e-14
(Table 2); degree 17 is kept.
